# Coslynx

**We make [Content Factory](https://coslynx.com): explainer reels made on your own computer, with your own Claude plan.**

Pick your channel. Content Factory finds the question your viewers want answered, researches it, narrates it and animates every frame, on your machine. Every number and quote is matched to its source before a word is recorded. No image or video generators: frames are drawn by its two film engines, in 90+ looks, or come from real, credited sources. Each reel is judged against hand-picked reels, and one under 8 out of 10 waits for you with the reason.

→ [coslynx.com](https://coslynx.com)

## Open source

Tools we built while making Content Factory, free under the MIT licence:

| | |
| --- | --- |
| [**video-safety-check**](https://github.com/CosLynxAI/video-safety-check) | Find flashes that can trigger seizures (WCAG 2.3.1), captions nobody can read and sudden loudness jumps (EBU R128) in any video, before you post it. |
| [**open-media-finder**](https://github.com/CosLynxAI/open-media-finder) | One search across Wikimedia Commons, NASA, the Smithsonian, three museums and Poly Haven, keeping only what each item's own licence lets you reuse, with credits written. CLI and MCP server. |
| [**ffmpeg-word-captions**](https://github.com/CosLynxAI/ffmpeg-word-captions) | Word-by-word captions (karaoke, highlight, reveal, pop) from a Whisper or other transcript, burned in with ffmpeg in one command. |
